## Discount Coupon Engine
#### Requirements
- we can add new coupons at runtime
- both cart level and product level discounts
- support multiple coupon types(seasonal offer, loyalty discount, Banking discount etc).
- support both flat and percentage discount and percentage with cap(of prize).
- one coupon can/cannot be applied on top of other coupons.
- thread safe.
non-functional are understood.
