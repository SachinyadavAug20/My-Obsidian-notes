### FDE(Forward deployed engineer)
here, deployed means put in a condition where there, is a problem to be solved.(close to custom, operation and management)
Define: A FDE works closely with a customer, client, department, or operation team. Then engineer observes the actual workflow, understands the constraints, builds the solution, and helps put it into real use.
Forward -> outside the product circle(create idea and frame correct solution -> proper requirement).
