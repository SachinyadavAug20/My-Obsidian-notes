## Decorator design pattern
need to enhance the properties/abilities.
will add layer on original object decorate it.
the outer layer can modify the output of the inner one.
![[Pasted image 20260930202450.png]]
enhance it ![[Pasted image 20260930202541.png]]

![[Pasted image 20260930205045.png]]
